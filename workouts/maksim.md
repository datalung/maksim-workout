# Full body

Maksim's program from the trainer — full body with bands and kettlebell,
cycling-specific core and hip work. Sequence matters: do not reorder.

## Warm-up #0033e6

### Jog in place, arms circling forward
scheme: 1 minute
segments: 60
trains: Shoulders, calves, pulse up

Quick small steps on your toes. Arms stretched out to the sides, rotating in small forward circles the whole time.

### Jog in place, arms circling backward
scheme: 1 minute
rest: 5
segments: 60
trains: Shoulders, calves

Same as before — quick steps on your toes — but rotate the straight arms in backward circles.

### Single-leg hops, band in front
scheme: 30 sec per leg
rest: 10
segments: 30 · Left leg, 30 · Right leg
trains: Balance, calves, shoulders

Hop in place on the toes of one foot. Hold the band stretched between both hands in front of you at shoulder height, arms spread, keeping tension.

### Single-leg hops, band overhead
scheme: 30 sec per leg
rest: 10
segments: 30 · Left leg, 30 · Right leg
trains: Balance, calves, shoulders

Same hops, but hold the band overhead with arms spread, keeping it under tension.

## Legs #e30613

### Bulgarian split squats
scheme: 2 × 15 per side
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 45
trains: Quads, glutes, balance

Back foot up on the couch behind you, front leg does the work. Lower until the front thigh is parallel to the floor, torso upright, then drive up through the front heel. All reps one side, then switch.

### Single-leg hip thrusts
scheme: 2 × 15 per side
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 45
trains: Glutes, hamstrings

On your back, knees bent. Lift one foot and extend that leg. Plant the working heel a little further from your hips than feels natural — that shifts more of the work into the hamstrings. Drive through the heel until shoulders–hips–knee form a line, squeeze at the top, lower with control.

### Quadruped hip extensions with band
scheme: 2 × 15 per side
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 45
trains: Glutes, lower back

On all fours, band looped around the sole of one foot, ends pinned under your hands. Push that leg straight back against the band until it's horizontal — no higher, and don't let the lower back arch or the hips tilt. Lower with control. All reps one side, then switch.

### Side-lying leg raises
scheme: 20 per side · band at ankles
reps: 1 × 20
rest: 30
trains: Outer hip, glute medius

Band around both ankles. Lie on your side, body straight, legs stacked. Lift the top leg straight up against the band, toes pointing forward, lower with control.

### Side-lying inner-thigh lifts
scheme: 20 per side
reps: 1 × 20
rest: 30
trains: Inner thigh, adductors

Lie on your side, head on your lower arm. Fold the top leg forward and plant its foot on the floor in front of the bottom knee. Keep the bottom leg straight and lift it a few centimeters off the floor, hold briefly, lower slowly. All reps one side, then turn over.

### Squats
scheme: 1 × 20
reps: 1 × 20
rest: 30
trains: Quads, glutes

Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Sit back and down, heels planted, chest up, thighs to parallel. Drive up through the heels.

## Core #ffc800

### Plank with leg raises
scheme: 2 × 45 sec
rest: 45
segments: 45, 45
trains: Deep core, glutes

Forearm plank, body in one line, core braced. Lift one leg a few centimeters, set it down, lift the other — alternating quickly for the full time. No sagging hips.

### Side planks
scheme: 2 × 30 sec per side
rest: 30
segments: 30 · Set 1 · left, 30 · Set 1 · right, 30 · Set 2 · left, 30 · Set 2 · right
trains: Obliques, lateral core, glute medius

Lie on your side, forearm under the shoulder, body in one straight line. Lift the hips and hold — no sagging, no twisting forward. This is what keeps your hips steady when climbing out of the saddle.

### Jackknives with band
scheme: 2 × 15
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 30
trains: Abs, hip flexors, shoulders

Flat on your back, legs straight, arms overhead holding the band spread under tension. Fold in half — straight legs and torso rise to meet — then lower with control. Keep band tension throughout.

### Russian twists
scheme: 1 × 20 · 4 kg kettlebell
reps: 1 × 20
rest: 30
trains: Obliques, rotation

Seated, knees bent, feet light or lifted, torso leaned back 45°. Kettlebell at your chest — rotate it to one hip, then all the way to the other. Twist from the waist, not the arms.

### Standing band rotations
scheme: 2 × 12 per side · band in door at shoulder height
reps: 2 × 12
rest: 30
trains: Obliques, rotation, trunk stability

Stand sideways to the door, feet shoulder-width, knees soft. Hold the band with both hands, arms straight out in front of your chest. Rotate the whole upper body away from the door — arms stay straight and in front of the chest, hips stay mostly square. Return slowly against the pull. All reps one side, then turn around.

## Upper body #0a0a0a

### Knee pushups on fists
scheme: 2 × 15
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 45
trains: Chest, triceps, front delts

Fists on the floor shoulder-width, knuckles down, wrists straight. Body in one line head to knees. Chest to just above the floor, elbows at 45°, press back up.

### Seated band rows
scheme: 2 × 15
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 30
trains: Lats, mid-back, biceps

Sit with legs extended, band looped around your feet, one end per hand. Sit tall and pull to the lower ribs, elbows driving back, shoulder blades squeezing together. Release with control.

### Superman hold with band
scheme: 45 sec
rest: 30
segments: 45
trains: Lower back, rear delts

Face down, fully stretched, arms straight ahead holding the band spread under tension. Lift arms, chest and legs off the floor and hold. Band stays taut.

### Prone arm circles
scheme: 1 × 15
reps: 1 × 15
rest: 30
trains: Rear delts, traps, mobility

Face down, arms straight ahead, thumbs up, chest slightly lifted. Sweep both arms in a wide symmetrical arc from overhead down to your hips and back — a slow breaststroke, arms never touching the floor.

### Band external rotations
scheme: 2 × 15 per side · band in door at belly height
reps: 2 × 15
rest: 30
trains: Rotator cuff, rear shoulder

Stand sideways to the door and hold the band in the hand farther from it. Elbow bent at a right angle, pillow squeezed between elbow and side. Rotate the forearm from the door outward, like a gate opening — the elbow stays pinned. Return slowly. Light band: this muscle is small and the slow way back matters most. All reps one side, then turn around.

## Stretch #00764a

### Banded hamstring stretch
scheme: 3 × 60 sec per side, deeper each stage
rest: 20
segments: 60 · Left leg · Stage 1, +60 · Left leg · Stage 2, +60 · Left leg · Stage 3, 60 · Right leg · Stage 1, +60 · Right leg · Stage 2, +60 · Right leg · Stage 3
trains: Hamstrings, calves

Lie on your back, one leg flat on the floor. Loop the band under the toes of the other foot and raise that leg vertical, holding the band with both hands. Stage 1: gentle pull, knee soft. Stage 2: straighten the knee fully and pull a little closer. Stage 3: pull further toward you and draw the toes down toward your face. Keep the bottom leg flat and the hips on the floor the whole time.

### Figure-4 glute stretch
scheme: 40 sec per side
rest: 10
segments: 40 · Left ankle up, 40 · Right ankle up
trains: Glutes, piriformis

On your back, knees bent. Cross one ankle over the opposite knee, then pull that thigh gently toward your chest until the outside of the crossed leg's hip stretches. Keep the back flat on the floor and the head down.

### Single-leg downward dog
scheme: 60 sec per side
rest: 10
segments: 60 · Left heel down, 60 · Right heel down
trains: Calves, Achilles, hamstrings

Hips up and back into an upside-down V. Hook one foot behind the other ankle so all the weight sits on one leg, then press that heel slowly toward the floor and hold. Knee straight, long back. Switch feet.
