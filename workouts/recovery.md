# Recovery

For the day after a long ride. Legs get nothing but stretching — the ride was
their workout. Light core, band pulling to open up the shoulders, and a long
stretch block against the riding position. About 20 minutes.

## Warm-up #0033e6

### March in place, arms circling forward
scheme: 1 minute
segments: 60
trains: Shoulders, circulation

Easy marching on the spot — no hopping today. Arms stretched out to the sides, rotating in small forward circles the whole time.

### March in place, arms circling backward
scheme: 1 minute
rest: 5
segments: 60
trains: Shoulders, circulation

Same easy marching, but rotate the straight arms in backward circles.

### Cat-cow
scheme: 45 sec
rest: 5
segments: 45
trains: Spine mobility

On all fours. Inhale: drop the belly, lift the chest and tailbone. Exhale: round the back up, tuck the chin. Slow waves, one breath per rep — wakes the spine up after hours bent over the bars.

## Core #ffc800

### Plank with leg raises
scheme: 2 × 30 sec
rest: 30
segments: 30, 30
trains: Deep core, glutes

Forearm plank, body in one line, core braced. Lift one leg a few centimeters, set it down, lift the other — alternating quickly for the full time. Shorter holds than usual — this is blood flow, not a test.

### Side planks
scheme: 30 sec per side
rest: 30
segments: 30 · Left, 30 · Right
trains: Obliques, lateral core

Lie on your side, forearm under the shoulder, body in one straight line. Lift the hips and hold — no sagging, no twisting forward. One set per side is enough today.

## Upper body #0a0a0a

### Seated band rows
scheme: 2 × 15
rest: 30
reps: 2 × 15
trains: Lats, mid-back, biceps

Sit with legs extended, band looped around your feet, one end per hand. Sit tall and pull to the lower ribs, elbows driving back, shoulder blades squeezing together. The direct antidote to hours hunched over the handlebars.

### Prone arm circles
scheme: 1 × 15
rest: 30
reps: 1 × 15
trains: Rear delts, traps, mobility

Face down, arms straight ahead, thumbs up, chest slightly lifted. Sweep both arms in a wide symmetrical arc from overhead down to your hips and back — a slow breaststroke, arms never touching the floor.

### Superman hold with band
scheme: 30 sec
rest: 30
segments: 30
trains: Lower back, rear delts

Face down, fully stretched, arms straight ahead holding the band spread under tension. Lift arms, chest and legs off the floor and hold. Band stays taut.

### Band external rotations
scheme: 1 × 15 per side · band in door at belly height
reps: 1 × 15
rest: 30
trains: Rotator cuff, rear shoulder

Stand sideways to the door and hold the band in the hand farther from it. Elbow bent at a right angle, pillow squeezed between elbow and side. Rotate the forearm from the door outward, like a gate opening — the elbow stays pinned. Return slowly. One light set per side today. Shut the band in the door so the door opens away from you.

## Stretch #00764a

### Banded hamstring stretch
scheme: 3 × 60 sec per side, deeper each stage
rest: 20
segments: 60 · Left leg · Stage 1, +60 · Left leg · Stage 2, +60 · Left leg · Stage 3, 60 · Right leg · Stage 1, +60 · Right leg · Stage 2, +60 · Right leg · Stage 3
trains: Hamstrings, calves

Lie on your back, one leg flat on the floor. Loop the band under the toes of the other foot and raise that leg vertical, holding the band with both hands. Stage 1: gentle pull, knee soft. Stage 2: straighten the knee fully and pull a little closer. Stage 3: pull further toward you and draw the toes down toward your face. Keep the bottom leg flat and the hips on the floor the whole time.

### Thread the needle
scheme: 40 sec per side
rest: 10
segments: 40 · Left, 40 · Right
trains: Upper back, shoulders

From all fours, slide one arm palm-up under your chest until the shoulder and ear rest on the floor. Let the upper back twist open. Directly targets the stiffness between the shoulder blades.

### Standing chest opener
scheme: 45 sec
rest: 10
segments: 45
trains: Chest, front shoulders

Stand tall, clasp your hands behind your back, straighten the arms and lift them gently away from your body while opening the chest and squeezing the shoulder blades. In a doorway: forearms on the frame, step through instead.

### Single-leg downward dog
scheme: 60 sec per side
rest: 10
segments: 60 · Left heel down, 60 · Right heel down
trains: Calves, Achilles, hamstrings

Hips up and back into an upside-down V. Hook one foot behind the other ankle so all the weight sits on one leg, then press that heel slowly toward the floor and hold. Knee straight, long back. Switch feet.
