# Workout Program

Full-body program built around unilateral (single-side) work, balance, and resistance-band exercises. **Do the blocks and exercises in this order** — warmup first, then legs (largest muscles), then core, then upper body, finishing with stretches. Within each block, the hardest compound exercises come first, so don't reshuffle.

Equipment: resistance band, 4 kg kettlebell.

---

## 1. Warmup

### 1.1 Jog in place with forward arm circles — 1 min
Jog in place with quick, small steps on your toes ("Tippelschritte"). Keep both arms stretched out straight to the sides and rotate them in small forward circles the whole time. Works up a light sweat, warms shoulders and calves.

### 1.2 Jog in place with backward arm circles — 1 min
Same as above, but rotate the straight arms in backward circles.

### 1.3 Single-leg hops with band pull-apart — per leg
Stand on one leg, hop in place on the toes of that foot. While hopping, hold the resistance band with both hands stretched out in front of you at shoulder height, arms spread so the band stays under tension. Repeat on the other leg.

### 1.4 Single-leg hops with overhead band hold — per leg
Same as above, but hold the band overhead with arms spread, keeping tension on the band. Repeat on the other leg.

> Note: the hopping on tiptoes doubles as calf training — that burn is real work, not just warmup.

---

## 2. Legs

### 2.1 Bulgarian split squats — 2 sets × 15 per side
Stand about one large step in front of a chair, bench, or couch, facing away from it. Place the top of one foot behind you on the bench. Lower yourself by bending the front knee until the front thigh is roughly parallel to the floor, keeping the torso upright, then push back up through the front heel. The front leg does the work; the back leg only balances. Complete all reps on one side, then switch.
*Trains: quads, glutes, balance.*

### 2.2 Single-leg hip thrusts — 2 sets × 15 per side
Lie on your back (or with upper back resting on a bench/couch edge), knees bent, feet flat on the floor. Lift one foot off the ground and extend that leg. Drive through the heel of the planted foot to lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulders to knee. Squeeze the glutes at the top, lower with control. All reps on one side, then switch.
*Trains: glutes, hamstrings.*

### 2.3 Single-leg Romanian deadlifts — 2 sets × 10 per side *(new)*
Stand on one leg with a soft bend in that knee, optionally holding the kettlebell in the opposite hand. Hinge forward at the hip — push your hips straight back and let the free leg extend behind you as a counterweight — until you feel a strong stretch in the back of the standing leg. Keep the back flat the whole time (don't round). Squeeze the glute to come back up. All reps on one side, then switch.
*Trains: hamstrings, glutes, balance. Added to fill the hip-hinge gap — and doubles as active flexibility work for stiff hamstrings.*

### 2.4 Side-lying leg raises (banded hip abduction) — 1 set × 20 per side
Loop the resistance band around both ankles. Lie flat on your side, body in a straight line, legs stacked and straight. Lift the top leg straight up against the band's resistance, keeping the leg straight and toes pointing forward (not up), then lower with control. All reps on one side, then switch.
*Trains: outer hip / glute medius.*

### 2.5 Bodyweight squats — 1 set × 20
Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out. Sit back and down as if onto a chair, keeping heels on the floor and chest up, until thighs are at least parallel to the ground. Drive back up through the heels.
*Trains: quads, glutes.*

---

## 3. Core

### 3.1 Plank with alternating leg raises — 2 sets × 45 seconds
Hold a forearm plank: elbows under shoulders, body in a straight line from head to heels, core braced. While holding, lift one leg a few centimeters off the ground, set it down, lift the other — alternating in quick succession for the full 45 seconds. Don't let the hips sag or pike up.
*Trains: deep core, glutes, shoulder stability.*

### 3.2 Jackknives with band tension — 2 sets × 15
Lie flat on your back, legs straight, arms extended overhead holding the resistance band with hands spread so it stays under tension. In one motion, lift your straight legs and your torso/arms toward each other, folding at the hips like a closing pocket knife, then lower back down with control. Keep tension on the band throughout.
*Trains: abs (full flexion), hip flexors, with shoulder tension work from the band.*

### 3.3 Russian twists — 1 set × 20 with 4 kg kettlebell
Sit on the floor, knees bent, feet lifted or lightly touching the ground. Lean the torso back ~45°, hold the kettlebell with both hands in front of your chest. Rotate the torso to bring the kettlebell beside one hip, then rotate all the way to the other side — that's the movement; keep alternating. Rotate from the waist, not just the arms.
*Trains: obliques, rotational core strength.*

---

## 4. Upper body

### 4.1 Knee pushups on fists — 2 sets × 15
Kneel and place your fists on the floor shoulder-width apart (knuckles down, wrists straight — the fist position keeps the wrists neutral). Body in a straight line from head to knees. Lower your chest to just above the floor, elbows at ~45° from the body, then press back up.
*Trains: chest, triceps, front shoulders.*

### 4.2 Seated band rows — 2 sets × 15 *(new)*
Sit on the floor with legs extended in front of you, loop the middle of the band around your feet, and hold one end in each hand. Sit tall, then pull the band toward your lower ribs, driving the elbows back and squeezing the shoulder blades together. Release forward with control, keeping the back straight.
*Trains: lats, mid-back, biceps. Added to balance all the pushing with a pulling movement.*

### 4.3 Superman hold with band — 1 set × 45 seconds
Lie face down, body fully stretched, arms extended straight in front of you holding the resistance band with hands spread to keep it under tension. Lift the arms, chest, and (slightly) the legs off the floor and hold, keeping the band taut the whole time.
*Trains: lower back, rear shoulders, upper back.*

### 4.4 Prone arm circles — 1 set × 15
Lie face down with arms extended straight in front of you, thumbs up, chest slightly lifted. In a wide, symmetrical circular motion, sweep both arms along the floor plane from overhead down to your hips (like a slow breaststroke), then return the same way. Keep arms lifted off the floor throughout.
*Trains: rear shoulders, traps, shoulder mobility.*

---

## 5. Stretching (cooldown) *(new)*

Static stretches go at the end, when the muscles are warm. Ease into each stretch — mild discomfort is fine, sharp pain is not. Breathe slowly; the stretch deepens on the exhale.

### 5.1 Lying hamstring stretch with band — 45–60 seconds per leg
Lie on your back, both legs on the floor. Loop the band around the sole of one foot and raise that leg straight up, holding the band ends. Keep the leg straight (soft knee is OK) and the other leg flat on the ground. Gently pull the band to bring the leg toward you until you feel a clear stretch in the back of the thigh. Hold, breathe, and let it deepen gradually.
*The safest and most effective hamstring stretch for stiff hamstrings — the floor keeps your back protected.*

### 5.2 Seated single-leg forward fold — 30–45 seconds per leg
Sit on the floor with one leg extended straight, the other bent with its foot resting against the inner thigh of the straight leg. Hinge forward from the hips (chest toward the toes, back long — don't round the spine to get lower) and reach toward the foot of the straight leg. Hold where you feel the hamstring stretch.

### 5.3 Downward dog — 45–60 seconds
From all fours, tuck the toes and lift the hips up and back so your body forms an upside-down V. Press the chest gently toward the thighs, and alternate "pedaling" the heels toward the floor one at a time. Knees can stay slightly bent — prioritize a long, straight back over straight legs.
*Stretches hamstrings and calves together — a good match for tight calves from all that tiptoe hopping.*
