Warmup:
- 1min "tippelschritte" (jogging in place?) while rotating strethced arms forward
- 1min "tippelschritte" (jogging in place?) while rotating strethced arms backward
- Repeat for both legs:
-- jump in place on one leg, arms spread with resistance band in front
-- jump in place on one leg, arms spread with resistance band above head

Legs:
- 2 sets of bulgarian squats, 15 per side
- 2 sets of 15 one legged hip thrusts, each leg
- 1 set of 20 per side: lie flat sideways, resistance band around ankles, lift straight leg
- 1 set of 20 squats

Core
- 2 sets of planks, lift legs in quick succession, 45 seconds each
- 2 sets of 15 jackknives (?), resistance band around arms, keep arm tension
- 1 set of 20 russian twists with 4kg kettlebell

Upper body
- 2 sets of 15 knee pushups on fists
- 1 set of 45 seconds lying face down, body stretched, arms held straight in front with resistance band for tension
- 1 set of 15: face down, symmetrical circular motion, arms in frton to hands on hip
